Privacy Policy

Easy Auto ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or apply for financing, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This is a general-purpose policy template and is not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having a lawyer review this policy to confirm it fully meets your specific obligations under PIPEDA and any applicable provincial privacy legislation before relying on it.

What information we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Application details: date of birth, employment status, employer name, income, housing situation, and self-reported credit information you provide through our application form
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site (including UTM tracking parameters and referral source), collected automatically as you browse
  • Communications: any messages you send us through our contact form, email, or phone

How we use your information

  • To assess your financing needs and connect you with lenders in our network who may be able to approve you
  • To contact you about your application, follow up on an inquiry, or respond to a message you sent us
  • To improve our website and understand which marketing efforts are working
  • To meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations

Who we share it with

We share your application information with lenders and financial institutions in our network for the purpose of finding you a financing offer. We also use third-party service providers to operate our business, including customer relationship management software, our web hosting provider, and communication tools. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Credit checks

An initial pre-qualification review typically does not require a hard credit inquiry. If you proceed with a specific lender's offer, that lender may need to run a credit check as part of their own approval process — they will inform you before doing so.

Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and as required to meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or ask us to delete your information, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand site usage and support advertising campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at hello@easyauto.ca or through our contact page.
